## 📽️ Slide 2: The Problem
### **The \$322B Silent Workplace Burnout Crisis**
* **76% of knowledge workers** report experiencing burnout at least once a quarter (Gallup / APA).
* **Meeting Fatigue & Fragmentation**: Remote workers average 21.5 hours of meetings weekly, leaving zero contiguous deep-work focus time.
* **The "Right-to-Disconnect" Breakdown**: Late-night Slack messaging and weekend PR reviews erode rest buffers without management noticing until an employee resigns.
* **Why Current Solutions Fail**:
  * ❌ *Quarterly HR Pulse Surveys*: Too late, filled out retrospectively, and dreaded by staff.
  * ❌ *Generic Wellness Apps (Calm / Headspace)*: Zero integration with actual work behavior.
  * ❌ *Employee Surveillance Tools*: Destroy trust, track keystrokes, and trigger quiet quitting.

---

## 📽️ Slide 3: The Solution
### **A Private, Self-Calibrating Digital Twin**
AI Wellness Twin creates a continuous, calibrated digital twin of every worker’s cognitive load and rest rhythms to detect burnout **2 to 4 weeks before exhaustion occurs**.

* **1. Multi-Modal Telemetry**: Passively aggregates workday signals across 9 tools (IDE, GitHub, Calendar, Slack, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.).
* **2. 28-Day Personal Calibration**: Evaluates workers exclusively against their own historical baseline—never ranked against peers.
* **3. "What-If" Twin Simulator**: Allows workers to simulate boundary changes (e.g., reducing meeting load by 20%) and forecast burnout recovery in real time.
* **4. Strict $k$-Anonymity ($k \ge 3$)**: Enables HR leadership to identify systemic team workload spikes while guaranteeing zero individual surveillance.

## 📽️ Slide 7: The Privacy Moat
### **Employee-First Trust Architecture**

1. **Zero Keystroke & Content Logging**: Prompts, code snippets, chat texts, and meeting titles are stripped at the local firewall.
2. **Self-Referential Only**: Workers are compared only against their own historical data—zero peer rankings or leaderboards.
3. **Bi-Directional Role Isolation**: Employees are strictly restricted from HR controls; HR admins are strictly blocked from viewing individual employee twins.
