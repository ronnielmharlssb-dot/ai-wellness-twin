1 Executive Summary

Knowledge workers in software engineering, design, and product development frequently suffer from invisible burnout. The modern workday is fragmented into overlapping video meetings, asynchronous chat interruptions, after-hours pull request reviews, and cognitive exhaustion. Conventional corporate wellness approaches rely on retrospective surveys filled out months after an employee has already burned out.

AI Wellness Twin resolves this dilemma by constructing a non-invasive, proactive digital twin of an individual worker's daily rhythms. By continuously synthesizing passive metadata timestamps (from tools like Visual Studio Code, GitHub, and Google Calendar) with active subjective pulse surveys (MBI-GS Maslach Burnout Inventory), the system alerts the employee to negative boundary erosion and provides actionable, simulated interventions before burnout occurs.

3 Supported Productivity Integrations

AI Wellness Twin connects with 9 essential workplace applications. In accordance with strict ethical standards, the metadata firewall extracts timestamps only and discards all sensitive text, code, and titles:

🐙 GitHub
Code & Development

Observes commit & PR review timestamps to measure active development hours and evening coding bursts.

💻 Visual Studio Code
Editor & Engineering

Measures uninterrupted focus blocks, keystroke pacing, and micro-pauses without reading source code.

🤖 ChatGPT (OpenAI)
AI Reasoning & Synthesis

Tracks AI-assisted deep work durations and session intensity. Prompt texts are strictly firewalled.

✨ Google Gemini
AI Research & Workflow

Monitors research session duration and multi-modal problem-solving intervals without recording prompts.

🧠 Claude (Anthropic)
AI Writing & Cognition

Observes long-form reasoning sessions and writing focus pacing with zero content logging.

📅 Google Calendar / Outlook
Calendar & Meetings

Computes meeting density, back-to-back fatigue, and schedule fragmentation. Meeting titles are discarded.

🎨 Figma
Design & Creative Flow

Measures design canvas active windows and creative deep work durations.

💬 Slack & Messaging
Team Communication

Monitors messaging timestamps outside core working hours to protect the employee's right-to-disconnect.

🎮 Discord
Voice & Community

Observes voice channel and community active windows to safeguard healthy evening shutdown habits.

4 Privacy & Ethical Safeguards

✓
1. Strict k-Anonymity (k ≥ 3): HR leadership can only view aggregate health insights for teams with 3 or more members. If a team has fewer than 3 members, aggregate stats are locked to prevent inferring an individual's state.
✓
2. Self-Referential Calibration Only: Employees are evaluated strictly against their own 28-day baseline. There are zero leaderboards, peer rankings, or productivity ratings.
✓
3. Zero Raw Content Ingestion: The metadata firewall processes timestamps and durations only. Chat messages, commit code, prompt text, and calendar titles are never parsed or stored.
