The Solution

A Continuous, Calibrated Digital Twin

1 Passive Telemetry Integration

Connects seamlessly with 9 tools (GitHub, VS Code, Slack, Calendar, ChatGPT, etc.) using a zero-text metadata firewall.

2 28-Day Personal Calibration

Evaluates employees strictly against their own historical baseline. Zero leaderboards, peer rankings, or competitive stress.

3 "What-If" Digital Twin Simulator

Interactive parameter simulation predicting recovery trajectories when meeting loads are reduced or focus blocks restored.

4 Strict k-Anonymity (k ≥ 3)

Mathematical privacy guarantee ensuring HR leadership can address team hazards while guaranteeing zero surveillance of individuals.
